# weekly-review

The OFP weekly review André runs to keep his life operating system aligned. Gaia-driven; other Primarchs may participate when scope demands.

**Status:** stub for v3.0. Codifies a process André currently runs manually.

## Intent

Once a week (typically end of week), surface:
- What got done
- What's open and aging
- What's drifting from priorities
- What needs a decision

So André can re-anchor his week and the agent system can re-align with reality.

## Inputs

- The week's `sessions.md` tail across relevant Primarchs
- `1 OFP/Threads/` and `Thread Index.md`
- Open items in `state.md` (Gaia and per-Primarch)
- André's calendar, if accessible

## Steps (TBD — refine after first run)

1. Pull sessions tail across Primarchs (Gaia first, then domain Primarchs as relevant).
2. Cross-reference open threads with state.md open loops.
3. Identify aging items (open > 2 weeks without movement).
4. Identify drift (energy spent on things outside stated priorities).
5. Produce a digest in markdown — sections for: progress, aging, drift, decisions needed.
6. Surface to André in conversation or write to inbox.

## Output

Markdown digest. Format TBD on first real run.

Refine after running it once with André. Don't over-design.
